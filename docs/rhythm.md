# Rhythm Patterns

Rhythm patterns define how many beats each resolved chord receives during progression playback. A unit represents one beat.

Examples:

- `2 2 4`: a two-bar `ii - V - I` cadence
- `4 4 4 4`: one full measure per chord in 4/4
- `1 2 4 8`: expanding durations

The available patterns and their display names are defined in `internal/music/rhythm.go`.
